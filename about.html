Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small dream in a home kitchen. Today, we are proud to be a community staple, providing our neighbors with the comfort of warm, handmade food.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe that great baking starts with great ingredients. That is why we source our flour from local, organic mills and our fruits from neighboring community farms.
